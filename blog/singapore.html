Singapore

September 2026

On the rare occasion I leave my vegetative state at my grandparents' house and venture down toward Marina Bay, I'm always impressed by the opulence Singapore boasts. The Louis Vuitton store looks as dazzling as the Louvre, sitting as one of many jewels lining Singapore's waterfront. Singapore's success as a country is unbelievable: with almost no land, freshwater, or natural resources, it grew in just 60 years from a nation whose own prime minister cried on national television, unsure it would survive, into a global hub of world-class governance and wealth — leveraging little more than its strategic position along the trade routes between China and India. Singapore is built on many humble beginnings (and still is) that I think most tourists don't associate with the country.
